SYRIA DYNAMIC TEMPLATE (SDT) - 12 MARCH 2017

SDT is the master mission of this project. Every flight is built here first and then shipped as a light mission of its own, for example SEA2STEEL (Sea to Steel). SDT keeps everything in one file: civil traffic (RAT, CAM), ambient radios, populated bases, the Skynet IADS and every support asset. It is heavier than any flight made from it.

COALITION
USS George Washington (CVN-73) in the eastern Mediterranean, Incirlik (LTAG) and Diyarbakir airfields, AWACS, tankers and fighter CAP.

HOSTILE
Skynet IADS with SA-2, SA-3, SA-6, SA-8, SA-10, SA-13, SA-15 and SA-19 sites and early warning radars. Populated bases with MANPADS and AAA. Fighter flights (MiG-23, MiG-29, Su-27, Su-33) launching from inland airfields. A-50 and IL-78 airborne.
